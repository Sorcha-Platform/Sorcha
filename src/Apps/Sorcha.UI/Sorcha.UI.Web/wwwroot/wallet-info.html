For citizens

Your credentials, on your device, in your control.

The Sorcha Wallet is an app you install on your phone. It holds the credentials organisations issue to you, and lets you present exactly what's asked for — and only that. Whoever you show them to can check they're genuine, without phoning anyone to confirm.

What you can do with it

  • Hold credentials offline. They live on your device, not on a server you have to trust.
  • Present only what's requested. Show a single field — a date of birth, a qualification, an entitlement — and keep the rest private. This is selective disclosure by design.
  • Let anyone verify. The party you present to can check the credential is genuine and unaltered, using cryptography rather than a phone call.

How it's different

The Sorcha Wallet is built on open standards — OpenID4VP for presentation and SD-JWT VC for the credential format — so it isn't a walled garden. The same standards the EU Digital Identity Wallet and GOV.UK Wallet are converging on are the ones the Sorcha Wallet speaks.

Get started

Open the wallet in your browser, or install it on your phone from the get-started page (it shows a QR code you can scan).
