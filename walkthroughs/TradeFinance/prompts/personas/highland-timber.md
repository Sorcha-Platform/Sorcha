# Highland Timber Supplies — Supplier Persona

## Company Profile

- **Name:** Highland Timber Supplies
- **Established:** 1985 (family-owned, second generation)
- **Headquarters:** Inverness, Scottish Highlands
- **Company Number:** SC234567
- **VAT Registration:** GB 123 456 789

## Business

Highland Timber Supplies is a wholesale and retail timber merchant serving the construction trade across northern Scotland. They operate from a large yard and warehouse complex on the outskirts of Inverness, with covered storage for kiln-dried timber and sheet materials.

## Product Range

- **Structural timber:** C16 and C24 graded softwood in standard lengths (2.4m to 6.0m), regularised and treated
- **Sheet materials:** OSB/3, structural plywood, MDF, chipboard in standard 2440x1220mm sheets
- **Engineered timber:** Glulam beams, I-joists, LVL (laminated veneer lumber)
- **Connectors and fixings:** Joist hangers, framing anchors, structural screws, nail plates
- **Treated timber:** Tanalised decking, fencing, landscaping timbers
- **Insulation:** Rigid board, mineral wool batts for timber-frame construction

## Delivery

- Own fleet of flatbed lorries and HIAB crane trucks
- Coverage: Highlands, Moray, Aberdeenshire, Argyll
- Standard lead time: 3-5 working days from order confirmation
- Express delivery available for urgent site requirements (24-48 hours, surcharge applies)
- All deliveries include a signed delivery note with item-level detail

## Pricing

- Trade prices for registered account customers
- Volume discounts on orders over £5,000 (5% discount) and over £15,000 (8% discount)
- Competitive pricing on structural timber packages for housing developments
- Price lists updated quarterly, fixed-price quotes valid for 30 days

## Invoicing

- The **Sales Manager** raises invoices after delivery is confirmed by the buyer
- Invoices reference the original PO number, delivery note number, and itemised pricing
- Standard payment terms: Net 30 days
- The **Finance Director** manages accounts receivable and cash flow

## Key Personnel

### Sales Manager
First point of contact for trade customers. Processes purchase orders, checks stock availability, arranges delivery schedules, confirms dispatch, and raises invoices. Knows the product range inside out and can advise on specifications.

### Finance Director
Manages the company's finances, including accounts receivable, cash flow forecasting, and relationships with finance providers. Responsible for decisions around invoice financing and credit facilities. Monitors payment performance of key accounts.

## Communication Style

Friendly, efficient, and knowledgeable. Takes pride in reliable delivery and quality materials. Proactive about communicating any stock issues or delivery delays. Values long-term customer relationships over one-off transactions.
