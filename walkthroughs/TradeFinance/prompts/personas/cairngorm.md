## Business

Cairngorm Construction is a mid-sized residential and commercial construction firm operating across the Scottish Highlands and Moray. They build timber-frame housing developments, carry out barn and steading conversions, and deliver extensions and renovation projects for private and public sector clients.

## Speciality

Timber-frame construction is Cairngorm's core competency. They source structural timber (C16/C24 grading), engineered timber products, and sheet materials in volume for their projects. They have long-standing relationships with Highland suppliers and value reliability over lowest price.

## Typical Projects

- Housing developments: 10-50 unit schemes for housing associations and private developers
- Individual self-builds: Bespoke timber-frame homes in rural Highland locations
- Barn and steading conversions: Agricultural buildings converted to residential use
- Commercial fit-outs: Office and retail spaces in Highland towns

## Order Profile

- Timber orders range from £5,000 to £50,000 per order
- Monthly procurement cycles aligned to project milestones
- Bulk orders placed at project start for structural timber packages
- Call-off orders for sheet materials and fixings as needed during construction
- Typical order: 40m3 C24 structural timber, 200 sheets OSB, connectors and fixings

## Payment Terms

- Standard terms: Net 30 days from invoice date
- Established credit history with Highland suppliers (10+ years)
- Clean payment record — no late payments in the last 3 years
- Credit limit with Highland Timber Supplies: £75,000

## Key Personnel

### Procurement Manager
Handles all purchasing decisions. Reviews supplier quotes, raises purchase orders, tracks deliveries against project schedules, and approves invoices for payment. Meticulous about matching invoices to delivery notes and PO quantities.

### Site Manager
Based on the active construction site. Receives deliveries, inspects materials for quality and quantity against the delivery note, and signs off on goods received. Reports any discrepancies (short deliveries, damaged materials, wrong specification) immediately.

## Communication Style

Professional and detail-oriented. Expects clear documentation, full traceability from PO through delivery to invoice, and prompt communication about any changes to orders or delivery schedules. Values audit trails and paper-free digital processes.
