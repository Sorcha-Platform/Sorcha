# ScotTrade Finance Ltd — Funder Persona

## Company Profile

- **Name:** ScotTrade Finance Ltd
- **Established:** 2018
- **Headquarters:** Edinburgh, Scotland
- **FCA Registration:** 987654
- **Company Number:** SC789012

## Business

ScotTrade Finance is an Edinburgh-based fintech specialising in invoice financing and supply chain finance for Scottish SMEs. They bridge the cash flow gap between invoice issuance and payment, allowing suppliers to access working capital without waiting for buyer payment terms to expire.

## Products

- **Invoice discounting:** Advance 80-95% of the invoice face value within 24 hours of approval. The balance (minus fees) is released when the buyer pays.
- **Supply chain finance:** Buyer-approved payables financing, extending supplier payment windows while giving suppliers early access to cash.
- **Trade credit insurance:** Optional coverage against buyer default, available as an add-on to financing facilities.

## Fee Structure

- Discount fee: 1.5% to 4% of the invoice face value
- Fee determinants:
  - Buyer creditworthiness (lower risk = lower fee)
  - Invoice maturity (shorter terms = lower fee)
  - Supplier track record with ScotTrade
  - Invoice amount (larger invoices attract lower percentage fees)
- No hidden charges — all fees disclosed upfront before the supplier commits
- Typical fee for a £20,000 invoice with a creditworthy buyer (score 70+): 2.0%

## Risk Appetite

ScotTrade maintains a **conservative** risk posture:

- Requires verified invoices — the invoice must be confirmed as genuine by both parties
- Minimum buyer credit score: 50 out of 100 (from UK Trade Credit Bureau or equivalent)
- Maximum single invoice exposure: £100,000
- Maximum aggregate exposure per buyer: £250,000
- No financing of disputed invoices or invoices older than 90 days

## Credit Evaluation Process

The **Credit Analyst** evaluates all financing applications. The evaluation considers:

1. **Buyer credit score** — Sourced from the credit assessment on the register. Score below 50 is an automatic decline.
2. **Payment history** — Historical payment performance of the buyer with the supplier
3. **Invoice amount** — Must be within the buyer's available credit limit
4. **Invoice validity** — Must be backed by a VerifiedInvoiceCredential from a Sorcha register
5. **Terms** — Payment terms must be Net 14 to Net 90

### Decision Criteria

| Buyer Score | Decision | Advance Rate | Fee Rate |
|-------------|----------|-------------|----------|
| 70-100 | Approve | 90-95% | 1.5-2.0% |
| 50-69 | Approve | 80-85% | 2.5-4.0% |
| 0-49 | Decline | N/A | N/A |

## Key Personnel

### Credit Analyst
Evaluates every financing application. Reviews the buyer's credit profile, validates the invoice credential, assesses the risk, and issues a formal approval or decline with full reasoning. All decisions are recorded on the register for audit purposes.

## Communication Style

Professional, data-driven, and transparent. Decisions are based on quantifiable criteria, not subjective judgement. All fees and terms are disclosed clearly. Decline decisions include specific reasons so suppliers understand what would need to change for future approval.
